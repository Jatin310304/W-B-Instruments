# How to add news, vlogs and job openings to the website

This guide is for office staff. You do not need any technical knowledge.

## 1. Open the content panel

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
2. Go to the panel address your IT person gave you. It looks like `https://www.your-website.com.au/your-secret-word/`.
   The panel is **not linked anywhere on the website**, so bookmark it.
3. Type your **user name** and **password** and click **Sign in**.

You stay signed in for 8 hours. Click **Sign out** (top right) when you are done on a shared computer.

## 2. Write a news post or a vlog

1. Click the **News & vlogs** tab, then **+ New post**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the headline, e.g. *W&B at Austech 2027*.
   - **Category**: Company announcement, Product launch, Exhibition, and so on.
   - **Date**: the date shown on the post. A future date keeps the post hidden until that day.
   - **Written by** (optional): your name or the team's.
   - **Short summary** (optional): one or two sentences shown on the news card. Leave it empty and the first paragraph is used.
   - **The post**: the text. Leave an **empty line** between paragraphs. Start a line with `- ` to make a bullet point.
3. **Picture**: every post shows a picture.
   - Click **Upload a picture** to use your own photo (JPG or PNG, up to 8 MB), or
   - click **Choose a standard picture** and click one of the website's own pictures.
   - If you do neither, a standard picture for the category is used, so a card is never blank.
   - Type a few words describing the picture (this helps blind visitors).
4. **Video (vlogs)**, optional:
   - Best: upload the video to YouTube (or Vimeo), copy the link from the address bar and paste it in **YouTube or Vimeo link**.
   - Or click **Upload a video** for a short MP4 file (up to 200 MB). Keep the page open until it says *Video uploaded ✓*.
5. Tick **Show on the website** and click **Save**. It is live straight away on the News page and the home page.
   Untick it to save a **draft** that only staff can see in the panel.

To change or hide a post later: click **Edit**, make the change (or untick *Show on the website*), and **Save**.
**Delete** removes it for good.

## 3. Add a job opening

1. Click the **Job openings** tab, then **+ New job opening**.
2. Fill in **Job title**, **Location** (e.g. *Kilsyth, VIC*), **Type** (Full-time, Part-time…), and optionally a **Closing date**.
3. **About the job**: what the person will do and what they need. Use `- ` at the start of a line for bullet points.
4. Optional: an email address where people can also send applications.
5. Tick **Show on the website** and click **Save**. It appears on the **Careers** page with an **Apply for this role** button.
   After the closing date it disappears from the website by itself.

Applications sent through the website arrive by email (with the CV attached) and also appear in the **Applications received** tab.

## 4. See enquiries and applications

- **Enquiries received**: every message sent from the website's contact form. Click a line to read it.
- **Applications received**: every job application, with a **Download CV** button.
- **Download as spreadsheet (CSV)** opens the list in Excel.

These contain people's personal details: do not forward or share them outside the team.

## Something went wrong?

- *"The user name or password is not right"*: check Caps Lock. After 10 wrong tries, wait 15 minutes.
- *"Please sign in again"*: your 8 hours ran out. Sign in again; nothing you saved is lost (anything not yet saved is).
- *A picture will not upload*: it must be JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF and under 8 MB. Photos straight from a phone are sometimes larger: send them to yourself by email at "medium" size first.
- *The change does not show*: refresh the website page (it can take up to a minute).

For new user accounts or a forgotten password, ask your IT contact (see "Admin accounts" in README.md).
