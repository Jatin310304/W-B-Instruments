# W&B Instruments website: what changed in v11

v11 applies the client feedback of November 2026 to v10 (second design option). Nothing else was changed: layout, colours, type, 3D models, navigation and all other content are as in v10.

## 1. Advanced Energy removed, Sensortherm added
W&B distributes Sensortherm (www.sensortherm.de/en/), so every Advanced Energy, Mikron, Impac and LumaSense reference and picture is gone, in all five languages (English, Italian, French, Arabic, Japanese).

- **Partner brand**: Sensortherm (Germany) replaces Advanced Energy on the home page, About page, Products filter and every product page. Its range list follows the Sensortherm website: pyrometers (1-colour and 2-colour), high-speed pyrometers and laser power control, temperature scanners, calibration sources and transfer standard pyrometers, PID controllers, displays and software.
- **Logo**: the Sensortherm logo is taken from Sensortherm's own datasheet artwork at high resolution, with a transparent background. The Advanced Energy logo file was deleted.
- **Infrared ranges**, rewritten from the Sensortherm site, each with a Sensortherm product photo and a link to the matching Sensortherm page:

| Range | Sensortherm products shown | Web address |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrared pyrometers | METIS, CAPELLA, FELIS | /products/ir-temperature |
| Pyrometers for metals (2-colour and short-wave) | CAPELLA C3, METIS M3 | /products/ir-metal |
| Pyrometers for glass applications | METIS M3 | /products/ir-glass |
| Pyrometers for non-metals and lower temperatures | FELIS F3, METIS M323 | /products/ir-non-metal |
| High-speed pyrometers and laser process control (new) | HELIOS H4, METIS H3 | /products/ir-high-speed |
| Infrared calibration sources | CS1500N, HE1200N, DIADEM | /products/ir-calibration-sources |
| Temperature scanners (new) | GALAXY | /products/ir-scanners |
| Pyrometer controllers and system components (new) | REGULUS, IF display, SensorTools | /products/ir-controllers |

  Three Advanced Energy-only ranges were replaced, because Sensortherm does not make them: semiconductor optical-fibre thermometry (now high-speed pyrometers), thermal imaging cameras (now GALAXY scanners) and petrochemical flame/flare monitoring (now controllers and system components).
- **Industry page**: "Semiconductors" (Advanced Energy only) is now **"Laser processing and additive manufacturing"** (/industries/laser-processing), Sensortherm's main high-speed application.
- **Copy updated** to match: home page intro, the temperature section, the "Why customers choose W&B" pillar, the two FAQ answers, the Infrared Thermometry and Calibration & Testing pages, page titles and descriptions for search, the search-engine company data in index.html and the trade-mark line on the Copyright page.
- "We represent manufacturers from Italy, the USA and the United Kingdom" now reads "Italy, Germany and the United Kingdom".

## 2. Product reference: HD31 Multifunction Logger
The calibration reference equipment now reads "Dry block calibrators and baths (Giussani), blackbody infrared calibration furnaces and the **HD31 Multifunction Logger (Delta OHM)**." It shows on the Calibration & Testing page, in all five languages.

## 3. Partner logos
- **Status Instruments** and **Giussani** use the logos the client supplied, trimmed and saved without quality loss. They are about 3 times the resolution of the v10 files (Status 196 × 48 px, was 73 × 18; Giussani 216 × 32 px, was 75 × 11).
- Senseca is unchanged.

## Please confirm
- **W&B's own calibration lab**: v10 said W&B uses *Mikron* blackbody calibration furnaces. The brand name was removed (the FAQ and the calibration equipment list now just say "blackbody calibration furnaces"). If the lab's furnaces are actually Sensortherm, we can name them.
- **Glass**: the Sensortherm site lists glass drop (gob) measurement as an application but shows no glass-specific model or photo, so the glass range shows a METIS M3 photo and links to Sensortherm's applications page.
- **Logo sharpness**: the supplied Status and Giussani files are small, so they look slightly soft on high-resolution (retina) screens. Vector (SVG or PDF) logos from the client would make them fully sharp.
- The product photos are Sensortherm's own. Check with Sensortherm that W&B may use them as a distributor (the usual arrangement).
