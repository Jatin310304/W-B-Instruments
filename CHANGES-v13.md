# W&B Instruments website: what changed in v13

v13 builds on v12 and applies the latest feedback. Layout, text and everything else are unchanged.

## 1. Our Businesses: product images instead of figures
The business cards no longer show headline figures (5 MT, 1–5000 kg, 0.5"–150", #2500). Each division now shows a product image taken from Metaval's own website. This applies to the Our Businesses page, the home page and the top of each division page.

| Division | Image | Source page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forging | Weld-neck flange | metaval.com/forging/ |
| Casting | Group of Metaval Alloys castings | metavalalloys.com |
| Flow | Electromagnetic flow meter | metaval.com/flow-measurement/ |
| Valves | Gate valve | metaval.com/valve/ |

The note under the image on each division page now reads "Image from Metaval's website" (in all five languages).

## 2. No more blank image tiles
The 7 products that showed an empty tile now show a photo from their own manufacturer's website:

| Product | Photo | Manufacturer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature transmitters | SEM203TC | Status Instruments |
| Signal conditioners | SEM1600T | Status Instruments |
| Temperature gauges (digital) | DM650TM | Status Instruments |
| Handheld thermometers | ECO120 | Senseca |
| Pressure measurement | HD2304.0 | Senseca |
| Thermal comfort | HD32.3TC | Senseca |
| Dry block calibrators | Giussani dry block calibrator | Giussani |

Sensortherm only makes infrared products, so its photos are used for the infrared ranges. Showing a Sensortherm pyrometer on a Status or Senseca product would show the wrong product. Every page was checked in light and dark mode, and no blank tile is left.

## 3. One background for every product image
- Every product photo has had its own background removed, so all of them, from Sensortherm, Status, Senseca, Giussani and Metaval, now sit on the same background.
- That background is the site's dot grid with a soft light behind the product. It is the same on product cards, range pages, product pages, industry pages and business pages.
- It switches with the mode: light grey with dots in light mode, deep navy with dots in dark mode. The white plates that v12 put behind photos in dark mode are gone.
- Four older, low-resolution Senseca pictures that had a blue photo backdrop (humidity, microclimate, water analysis, and an outdoor weather-station photo) are replaced with Senseca's current product renders: HD2101.2, HD32.2, ECO521 and the HD9007 radiation shield. The panel-meter photo (DM3410) is replaced with a sharper copy from Status.

## Also fixed
- The Senseca "visit the manufacturer" links pointed to deltaohm.com, which now shows a security-certificate error. They now point to the matching pages on senseca.com.
